# Tekriwal independent design proposals

Original HTML/CSS/JavaScript and procedural vehicle geometry by Durgesh Kumar. This is an independent proposal, not a commissioned or official Hero/Tekriwal website.

The motorcycles catalogue reflects 20 Hero-branded motorcycle entries in Hero MotoCorp's official India navigation, observed 21 October 2026. Product and photograph URLs link to those exact official sources. Detailed specification rows prefer the individual official model page; when dynamic data could not be verified, only sourced range facts and the complete official product link are shown. National model listing does not establish Godda stock, exact variants, retail price or production status.

Official product photos and Xtreme 250R film load from Hero's domains. They remain third-party media; no ownership or reuse licence is claimed. No Porsche media, code, font or branding is bundled: its public site informed only the broad image-led presentation direction.

The original generic 3D illustration is not a replica or a photograph of any particular Hero model. Three.js 0.170.0 loads from the pinned jsDelivr npm distribution; Three.js is MIT licensed. Fonts load through Google Fonts with Arial fallback.

Enquiry preparation and comparison run locally. Copying requires a visitor click. The outgoing official dealership links open separately; no personal details, enquiry, booking, order or payment are submitted by this page. Reduced-motion styles retain photographs and readable content without video or WebGL motion.
