# Tekriwal motorcycle assets

Current opening: original Hero Xtreme 250R banner footage, edited to omit plate-bearing close shots. Native 1440×378 dimensions/aspect are retained; no claim of restored 4K detail. CRF16 derived cut, muted. No generated 125R dashboard or blue-bike scene is mixed into the 250R film.

Four scroll-controlled chapters connect introduction, cinematic atmosphere, model details and a showroom shortlist. A separate full-screen player preserves the full frame. Reduced-motion visitors receive a static, readable story.

Hero product photographs and footage remain owned by their respective rights holders. This independent design proposal is not a commissioned or official dealer website. Enquiry handoff opens the existing official route; this site submits no messages, orders, bookings or payments.
